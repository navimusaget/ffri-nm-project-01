# FFRI-NM Project 01 — WHEN AI BECOMES EVIDENCE

**When AI Becomes Evidence: A Comparative Audit of Machine-Signal Disclosure in University Academic Integrity Procedures**

Research Note 01 · Version 1.0 · Published 21 September 2026

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22879568

Institutional context: **Foundation for Relational Inquiry (FFRI-NM)**  
Author: **Navi Musaget**

## About

This research note audits public academic-integrity procedures at 24 purposively selected universities in Australia, Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom.

It examines how AI-detector outputs move from technical signals into institutional evidence, what part of the signal becomes visible to the affected student, when disclosure occurs, and how the signal can be challenged.
