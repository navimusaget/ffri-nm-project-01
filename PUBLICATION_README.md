# FFRI-NM Project 01 — WHEN AI BECOMES EVIDENCE

**Research Note 01 · Version 1.0 · Public release · 21 September 2026**  
Institutional author: **Foundation for Relational Inquiry (FFRI-NM)**

## Title
**When AI Becomes Evidence: A Comparative Audit of Machine-Signal Disclosure in University Academic Integrity Procedures**

## Short description
This research note audits public academic-integrity procedures at 24 purposively selected universities in Australia, Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom. It examines how AI-detector outputs move from technical signals into institutional evidence, what part of the signal becomes visible to the affected student, when disclosure occurs, and how the signal can be challenged.

The study separates tool-layer visibility, formal evidence disclosure, human interpretation, signal-level contestability and decision-level review. It is descriptive rather than normative: it does not estimate prevalence, rank institutions, assess legal compliance, or test detector accuracy.

## Method and scope note
The 24-university corpus is an exploratory maximum-variation purposive sample and is not representative of higher education as a whole. Counts describe the corpus only. `NOT FOUND` is not coded as `NO`. Local rules are not silently generalized to the institution. Public-source URLs and audit dates are included in the companion workbook; a complete snapshot archive is not redistributed in this release.
